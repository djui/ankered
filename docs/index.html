Live monitoring

Every port, at a glance.

Click the bolt in the menu bar. Total watts stay in the status item while you work. The popover lists C1–C3 with live watts, volts, and amps.

  • Cable & profile E-Marker ratings, PD Fast Charging, and connected device names from a local USB VID/PID table.
  • Port nicknames Label C1 as MacBook, C2 as iPhone — stored only on this Mac.
  • Shutdown timers 1h / 2h / 3h presets or a custom countdown, plus output on/off with a confirm step.

Privacy

Stays on this Mac.

All charger telemetry and history stay on-device. Up to 24 hours of samples and last-known charger identity live in Application Support. Port nicknames live in UserDefaults. The app makes no network requests.

  • No Anker account and no cloud protocol management.
  • Diagnostics Copy All omits serials, session keys, and decrypted payloads.

Mac convenience

Shortcuts, login, idle notify.

Open at login. Get a notification when a port sits at 0 W for 10 minutes. Use Shortcuts to turn C1–C3 on or off while the app is running.

  • Reconnect remembers the last charger UUID.
  • Fault banners for over-temperature and port abnormalities.
